How to play

Drag a glowing hand card onto a green cell to play it, or click it and then click a cell. Gold cells give it a bonus there (+N = Attack + Health it adds to your board).
In combat, drag a glowing unit onto a red-outlined enemy unit or hero to attack.
In your main step, drag a Redeploy unit to an empty cell; click a ⚡ unit to activate it.
When attacked: click a gold blocker (or No block), then a gold Tactic (or No response).
zZ = placed this turn. Front zone blocks; side zone flanks.
Hero power: each turn your opponent gets damage past your shield, you gain a charge. A gold button spends 1 charge and 2 resource to use your hero's power (once a turn). The more units you are behind, the stronger it is.
